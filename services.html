Our Services

Global Reach

In addition to focusing on the efficient operation and management of its fleet, primarily across Asia—including the Middle East and Northeast Asia—HN SHIPPING & TRADING CO., LTD actively expands its business through chartering and cargo brokerage services, with a trading range that extends to Africa and a clear ambition to further expand its global reach.

Flexible Approach

With a flexible and market-oriented approach, the company not only optimizes the performance of its owned vessels but also leverages its network to connect cargo opportunities with suitable tonnage. Through chartering and brokerage activities, HN SHIPPING & TRADING CO., LTD continuously broadens its customer base and strengthens its presence within the regional and international shipping markets.

Sustainable Growth

Driven by a long-term vision and a commitment to sustainable growth, the company aims to further diversify its service offerings, expand its operational scope, and enhance its position in the international maritime transport industry.
